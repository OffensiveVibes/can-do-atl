# Can Do ATL — Design Direction

## Three initial directions

### Theme Name: Neighbourhood Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** A friendly community bulletin rendered as a tactile collection of field notes, hand-drawn marks, and handmade paper. It makes mutual aid feel local, active, and unmistakably student-led.

**Probability:** 0.06

### Theme Name: Campus Pantry Almanac
**Very Brief Intro:** Editorial and orderly, with collegiate typography, harvest-colored data markers, and a calm service-journal feel. It would make resources feel trustworthy and easy to navigate.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Shared Table Studio
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, expressive system inspired by community kitchens and student zines, using loose illustrated ingredients and curved, room-like content blocks. It foregrounds care and collective action over institutional formality.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Neighbourhood Field Notes

### Design Movement
**Contemporary civic zine** meets **handmade community noticeboard**. The site will feel like a thoughtfully assembled collection of campus flyers and illustrated notes rather than a generic nonprofit template.

### Core Principles
1. **Care made tangible:** tactile paper textures, playful linework, and human-scale language suggest that every donation and volunteer hour reaches a person.
2. **Purposeful asymmetry:** stacked notes, off-set content blocks, and collage-inspired scenes keep the page energetic without compromising clarity.
3. **Action stays obvious:** volunteer and donate routes remain high contrast, visible early, and repeated at meaningful decision points.
4. **Campus-spanning community:** Georgia Tech, Georgia State, and Kennesaw State are treated as connected neighbors, not isolated audiences.

### Color Philosophy
Cream (#F7F3EB) is the quiet paper ground that reduces stress. Forest green (#3A5A40) anchors trust, nourishment, and practical action. Terracotta (#BC6C25) acts as the warm human signal for momentum, calls to action, and hand-drawn details. Warm charcoal (#2C2C2C) gives editorial readability without the harshness of black.

### Layout Paradigm
The page follows a **community-board trail**: a left-weighted hero note, a row of illustrated service cards, a long impact strip, and a testimonial/postcard pause before the footer. Content blocks overlap gently and use varied paper edges to avoid a uniform card grid.

### Signature Elements
1. Rough, hand-drawn loops, dividers, and orbit lines around important stories and statistics.
2. A seed/leaf/basket symbol used as the header mark and visual anchor.
3. Cream paper panels with selective torn-edge and taped-note details, never used as ornamental noise.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel responsive and inviting: CTAs compress slightly when pressed, navigation scrolls to its section, and the testimonial carousel advances with clear, accessible controls. No interaction should obscure the primary mission or add a dependency on complex behavior.

### Animation
Use brief, low-key motion only: content lifts or shifts by a few pixels on hover; the hero illustration drifts imperceptibly on wide screens; testimonial transitions use opacity and a shallow horizontal slide under 300ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by making all nonessential movement instant.

### Typography System
**Fraunces** will provide the warm, expressive serif display voice for headlines, callouts, and oversized numbers. **Manrope** will provide body, navigation, buttons, and utility text for contemporary clarity. Headline line-height remains compact; body copy is airy and easy to scan.

### Brand Essence
**Can Do ATL connects campuses to practical, dignified access to food and clothing through collective student action.** Personality: **grounded, generous, resourceful**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound direct and hopeful; CTAs are clear invitations to participate; microcopy is candid about practical needs. Avoid generic startup language.

> “Small supplies. Shared strength.”

> “Bring what you can. Take what helps.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a bold, hand-drawn **open basket with a sprouting leaf**—a symbolic place where essentials and care are shared. The accompanying wordmark uses a confident soft-serif treatment with a tiny dot/seed motif.

### Signature Brand Color
**Can Do Terracotta — #BC6C25.**

## Style Decisions

- Use a generous cream canvas, never pure white, with forest-green content anchors and terracotta conversion moments.
- Keep social links in a clearly marked footer area with editable placeholders for LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube.
- Favor illustrations and linework in the visual hierarchy, with readable text backgrounds throughout.
- No implementation-facing language appears in the public design; visible copy stays grounded, practical, and student-led.
- Each major section must carry at least one field-note cue, such as tape, a dotted trail, offset layering, or hand-drawn annotation.
- Georgia Tech, Georgia State, and Kennesaw State are presented as one connected campus trail, not isolated locations.
